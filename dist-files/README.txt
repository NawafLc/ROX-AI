ROX-AI  -  portable app
=======================

Double-click  ROX-AI.exe  . A setup wizard walks you through everything.

- Nothing is installed: everything (settings, history, your Obsidian vault) lives in this folder.
  Put the folder anywhere you like - Desktop, Documents, a USB stick.
- The app checks what you already have (Obsidian, Roblox, Claude) and shows a one-click
  button only for what's missing.
- Use an ALT Roblox account, never your main.
- To stop the AI while it plays: touch your mouse/keyboard, press Ctrl+Shift+Q, or click Stop.
- Updates: a yellow "Update" button appears in the app when a new version is out.
- To remove: in the app click More > Remove from this PC, then delete this folder.

Files:
  ROX-AI.exe      the app (double-click this)
  roxai.exe       helper that Claude talks to (leave it next to ROX-AI.exe)
  data\           created on first start: settings and history
  ROX-AI Vault\   created on first start: your Obsidian vault
